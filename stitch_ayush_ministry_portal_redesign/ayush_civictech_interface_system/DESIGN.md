## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the rigor, institutional gravitas, and civic trust of the Ministry of AYUSH, Government of India. Designed for public-sector enterprise workflows, blind evaluation platforms, and national skill registries, the visual language balances constitutional authority with modern civic-tech efficiency. It eliminates arbitrary decoration, neon gradients, and tech gimmicks in favor of grounded structure, purposeful framing, and absolute clarity.

The target audience spans ministry officials, healthcare recruiters, certified AYUSH practitioners, accredited educational institutions, and candidates across India. The emotional response is immediate trust, administrative credibility, impartiality, and unyielding accessibility.

The aesthetic philosophy draws from **Modern Civic Brutalism** blended with **Classical Institutional Rationalism**:
- **High-contrast structural framing:** Razor-sharp layout boundaries, solid institutional borders, and systematic tabular hierarchy ensure data integrity.
- **National heritage palette:** Deep National Forest Green anchors the system with longevity, complemented by Imperial Gold/Ochre accents representing Ayurvedic legacy and state distinction.
- **Strict Information Density:** Designed to display high volumes of critical regulatory data, NSQF qualification tiers, blind evaluation metrics, and verified candidate rosters without visual fatigue.

## Colors

The color palette is rooted in institutional authority, national identity, and high-readability compliance (WCAG AAA standard for body copy and core data).

- **Primary (`#0F5132` - National Forest Green):** Represents the Ministry of AYUSH, clinical certification, and botanical/holistic healthcare integrity. Used for primary mastheads, high-level action buttons, key qualification badges, and top-tier metrics.
- **Secondary (`#B45309` - Imperial Gold / Deep Ochre):** Signifies national accreditation, state honors, caution/notice frames, and NSQF level highlights. Delivers an authoritative civic accent without the frivolity of bright saturated yellow.
- **Tertiary / Core Ink (`#0F172A` - Slate Charcoal):** High-density neutral ink applied to all structural typography, data tables, labels, and borders requiring razor-sharp definition.
- **Neutral Surface (`#F8FAFC` - Slate Off-White):** Provides an institutional, glare-reducing foundation. Paired with pure white (`#FFFFFF`) card containers and crisp border neutrals (`#E2E8F0` and `#CBD5E1`) to delineate dense information segments.

### Status & Verification Roles
- **Verified / Qualified:** `#0F5132` (Forest Green background at 10% opacity, solid `#0F5132` border and glyph).
- **Advisory / Draft:** `#B45309` (Amber Ochre with solid `#B45309` edge).
- **Institutional Alert:** `#991B1B` (Deep Saffron Red, reserved for system alerts and compliance rejections).
- **Subdued Meta Neutral:** `#64748B` for secondary annotations, timestamps, and NSQF sub-codes.

## Typography

The typography architecture marries **Public Sans** (developed explicitly for governmental and administrative clarity) with **Inter** (for dense data rendering and micro-legibility). 

- **Public Sans (Headlines, Sub-headers, Official Labels):** Provides an uncompromised, civic presence reminiscent of official gazettes and statutory portals. Uppercase tracking is applied to micro-labels, NSQF level markers, and ministry department headers (`letterSpacing: 0.06em` to `0.08em`) to enforce structural authority.
- **Inter (Body, Submission Texts, Tabular Data):** Leveraged for candidate dossiers, clinical job descriptions, syllabus requirements, and tabular columns. Inter's tall x-height and distinct numerical glyphs prevent misinterpretation of hours, percentages, and verification IDs.
- **Data Display Rules:** Metrics such as merit percentages, NSQF hours, and cohort stats must use standard tabular lining numbers to ensure clean vertical alignment across rows.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column rigid responsive grid system engineered for enterprise dashboards, multi-candidate verification sheets, and high-density tabular reviews.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and a max content width capped at `1280px` for optimal ocular tracking across data panels. Section margins hold at `2rem` (32px).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.5rem` margins. Complex dual-pane recruitment split views reflow into stacked cards.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4 columns with `0.75rem` gutters and `1rem` outer margin. Badges, skill tags, and action buttons collapse into systematic full-width stacks.

### Density and Rhythmic Cadence
The system enforces strict layout compacting:
- **Card and Row Padding:** Dense data rows utilize `space-sm` (8px) vertical padding; major record cards utilize `space-md` (16px) or `space-lg` (24px).
- **Administrative Header Strips:** Statutory notice boxes and official disclaimers maintain a rigid 1px solid left accent bar with `space-md` interior clearance.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids soft, floating, or dramatic drop shadows. In an authentic governmental and civic-tech context, depth must imply structural stability, auditability, and physical document order rather than virtual lightness.

### Structural Grounding via Borders
- **Zero Heavy Shadows:** Shadows are replaced by crisp, 1px solid structural borders (`#E2E8F0` on base surfaces; `#CBD5E1` on interactive surfaces).
- **Ambient Micro-Elevation:** When surface elevation is mandatory (e.g., sticky action toolbars or active candidate modals), a razor-thin, cold ambient shadow is used: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Administrative Left-Bar Border Accent:** Cards containing high-priority institutional context or statutory warnings incorporate a solid 3px or 4px left border (`#0F5132` for verified/active, `#B45309` for advisory/provisional).
- **Surface Nesting:** Base page canvas sits on `#F8FAFC`. Contained modules, candidate dossiers, and syllabus breakdowns occupy `#FFFFFF`. Secondary sub-containers (e.g., raw submission review panes) sit on `#F1F5F9` with complete border containment.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Level 1 (Soft)** roundedness. All core interactive and static containers strictly utilize `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii (`rounded-sm`), with outer parent cards never exceeding `0.375rem` (6px). 

- **Rationale:** High-radius or pill shapes degrade the institutional seriousness of civic data platforms and consume unnecessary screen real estate in dense tables. Sharp, minimal 4px corners evoke official ledgers, certificates, and formal registries.
- **Qualification & Skill Chips:** 4px radius with 1px border.
- **Buttons and Inputs:** 4px radius, providing a crisp, chiseled administrative edge.
- **Avatar & Candidate Badges:** Blind evaluation candidate markers (e.g., `#1`, `#2`) are rendered in crisp rounded squares rather than circular consumer avatars, signaling blind merit anonymity.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary Civic Button:** Solid `#0F5132` (National Forest Green) background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.25rem` radius, 1px solid `#0F5132`. Hover state shifts to `#0A3622`. Focused with a 2px offset ring in `#B45309`.
- **Secondary / Action Outline Button:** Transparent background, 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`. On hover: surface tints to `#F1F5F9` with border `#94A3B8`.
- **Institutional Toggle Button (e.g., Raw vs. Blind Merit View):** Segmented container with `#F1F5F9` background, 2px internal padding. The active segment features a solid `#0F5132` or `#FFFFFF` fill with 1px border and high-contrast text.

### NSQF Tags, Badges & Verification Chips
- **Status Badges:** Compact Public Sans uppercase text (`label-md`).
  - *Verified Competency:* Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#0F5132`, border 1px solid `#A7F3D0`. Prefixed with a formal check glyph.
  - *Pending / Advisory:* Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, border 1px solid `#FDE68A`.
  - *NSQF Level Tag:* Crisp `#0F172A` text on `#F8FAFC` background, bordered with `#E2E8F0`.
- **Skill Competency Chips:** Height 24px, font size 12px, background `#F1F5F9`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, text `#334155`. When verified/evaluated: shifts to `#E6F4EA` with `#0F5132` border.

### Candidate & Merit Record Cards
- **Structure:** Solid white (`#FFFFFF`) card with 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border and `0.25rem` corner radius.
- **Header Strip:** Left side displays blind sequence badge (e.g., `#01`, `#02`) in bold `#0F5132` ink inside an off-white box; candidate name and verified institution are arranged hierarchically in Public Sans. Right side anchors the primary action button (`Shortlist` or `Verify Qualification`).
- **Metric Indicator:** Prominent data block featuring merit matching percentage (e.g., `85%`) in bold 24px tabular font, paired with evaluated skill fractions (`(4/5 skills)`).
- **Verification Statement Block:** Submissions sit within an indented neutral enclosure (`#F8FAFC`) with a subtle 2px left border in `#CBD5E1` and micro-label `OFFICIAL SUBMISSION / PROOF RECORD`.

### Data Tables & Curriculum Indicators
- **Header:** Uppercase `label-md` tracking, background `#F1F5F9`, 1px solid border at bottom `#CBD5E1`.
- **Demand Trend Progress Bars:** 6px track height, neutral gray track (`#E2E8F0`), filled strictly with solid `#0F5132` (or `#B45309` for niche categories). Zero rounded endcaps; clean rectangular meters.

### Form Inputs & Filters
- **Text Inputs & Dropdowns:** 36px height for high efficiency. Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`. Focus state applies a 1px border in `#0F5132` and an outer 2px subtle ring in `rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.15)`.
- **Statutory Notice Banners:** Prominent alert strip with 4px solid left accent in `#B45309`, light background `#FFFBEB`, and dark amber administrative text detailing compliance requirements and demo disclaimers.
